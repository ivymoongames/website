02 · DESIGN INTENT

Guide first. Then let the player wander.

The critical path remains legible, while optional locations provide reasons to explore and return to the larger objective with more context, abilities, or resources.

01

Guided arrival

Intro cinematic → camera control → movement control → Longhall Pier. The opening establishes place and orientation before asking for decisions.

02

Discovery space

Ravens, fishing, the Marauder's ship, Old Watchtower, shops, shrine, and worker areas create optional destinations around the main route.

03

Meaningful choices

Glima combat and the Gated Bridge introduce choices that are framed as alternatives rather than arbitrary quest branches.

04

World-state escalation

Lighting the ancient forge leads toward a boss encounter and a return to the surface as Ragnarok begins to change the world.

03 · GAMEPLAY FLOW

Branching beats, one larger arc.

The experience moves from onboarding to exploration, then gradually concentrates the player toward Halvard's station and the forge.

04 · THE TURNING POINT

Light the forge.
Change the world.

The current exercise doesn't need to fully solve the post-forge threat. The stronger design move is establishing the setup: the forge is awakened, the player understands something larger has been released, and the world has entered a new state.
