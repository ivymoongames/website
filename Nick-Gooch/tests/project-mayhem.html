A world built around

A compact Viking-fantasy gameplay space organized around a guided arrival, optional exploration, social choices, and a major world-state escalation at the ancient forge.

01 · THE WORLD

Where do you go when no one tells you where to go?

You arrive by boat and take in the settlement from the Longhall. Key places are visible from the start, giving the player several reasons to set out and see what they find.

World map and gameplay guide - current version, 10/12/2026.
02 · THE IDEA

Give the player a reason to look around.

People, places, and small mysteries pull the player through the frozen settlement. The player can follow whatever catches their attention and gradually piece together what is happening.

01

Arrive somewhere real

The boat reaches Longhall Pier. The player gets control gradually, sees the settlement, and can start making their own way through it.

02

Follow the interesting thing

A strange sword in the snow, a fisherman, ravens overhead, a ship on the ridge, workers in trouble - each offers a different reason to wander.

03

Let choices change the journey

A fight in the Longhall and a confrontation at the bridge give the player meaningful choices. Those decisions can come back later in the journey.

04

Build toward one big question

Everything eventually points toward Halvard's ancient forge. Why was it built? Why was it sealed? And what happens when it burns again?

03 · THE JOURNEY

A freeform beginning builds toward a clear destination.

The player starts with freedom to explore. Each discovery draws them closer to Halvard's ancient forge.

04 · THE TURN

The forge wakes.
Something wakes with it.

Relighting the ancient forge sets the final turn in motion. Its fire breaks through the ice and wakes something that has been waiting beneath it. On the journey home, the player sees the first signs of Ragnarok taking shape around them.
